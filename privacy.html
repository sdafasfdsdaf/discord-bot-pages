Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Discord application “Discord Bot” (the “Application”) may process and how that information is used.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the Application’s features, information provided through the Discord API may be processed. This may include Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role information, and information related to interactions with the Application’s commands.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to provide the Application’s features, process commands, apply server settings, and maintain the Application’s functionality.

3. Data Storage

If the Application stores information, it is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant feature to operate. Information that is no longer needed should be deleted within a reasonable period.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and services required to operate the Application and may be transmitted through the Discord API as part of Discord’s services.

5. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will always be published on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact information provided on the Application’s Discord page.
